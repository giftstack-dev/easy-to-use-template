HOW TO ADD PHOTOS & USE PRINT-TO-CV IN GRADUATE
================================================
1. ADDING YOUR PHOTOS
---------------------
- Save your profile photo and project screenshots in this folder (e.g. portrait.jpg, project1.jpg).
- Open index.html in any text editor.
- Locate the <img> tags with data-photo="..." or the project thumbnail slots.
- Replace the demo URL with your local image:
    src="images/portrait.jpg"
- The built-in layered image system uses an SVG monogram background, so your portfolio
  looks elegant and professional even before your images finish loading!

2. HOW TO EXPORT YOUR PRINTABLE ONE-PAGE CV / RESUME
---------------------------------------------------
- Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- Press Ctrl + P (or Cmd + P on Mac), or choose "Print" from your browser menu.
- In the Print dialog:
  * Destination: "Save as PDF"
  * Layout: Portrait
  * Paper size: A4 or Letter
  * Margins: Default or Minimum
  * Options: Check "Background graphics"
- The template's custom @media print stylesheet will automatically format your profile,
  hiding the navigation bar, buttons, and floating WhatsApp widget, producing a clean,
  recruiter-ready single-page resume with black text on white paper!
