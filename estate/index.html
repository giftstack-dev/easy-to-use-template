The Lekki Prime Trust Standard

Nigerian real estate should be an exhilarating wealth multiplier, never an anxiety-ridden risk. Here is how we protect your capital.

Guaranteed Title Verification

Every property undergoes legal search at the Lands Registry (Alausa & State Registry) to confirm Governor's Consent, C of O, or Gazette before listing. Zero family land disputes (Omonile).

0% Buyer Commission

When buying brand-new builds or developer off-plan projects through our advisory, you pay zero agency fees. We are compensated directly by vetted developers.

Diaspora Investment Concierge

Over 60% of our buyers live in the UK, USA, and Canada. We provide live 4K virtual inspections, legal deed execution by power of attorney, and trusted tenant management.

Essential Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I receive upon purchasing a property?
Upon full payment, you receive a Deed of Assignment signed by the developer or legal owner, a Registered Survey Plan in your name, the original Title Deed (or certified true copy of Governor's Consent / C of O), and a formal Letter of Allocation.
Do you offer flexible payment plans or mortgages?
Yes! For off-plan and new build properties, our developer partners offer structured payment installments across 6 to 18 months tied to construction milestones. We also partner with leading Nigerian mortgage institutions for qualified salary earners.
Can you manage the property and find tenants for me?
Yes, through our Lekki Prime Asset Management division, we screen verified corporate tenants, collect annual rents in Naira or USD, handle maintenance, and remit proceeds directly to your nominated bank account.
