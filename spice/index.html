Frequently Asked Questions

How much advance notice is required for party catering?
For weekend party coolers and packages under 50 guests, we require 48 hours notice. For weddings and corporate banquets exceeding 100 guests, we advise locking down your date at least 2 weeks in advance to secure equipment and food styling staff.
Do you cater events outside Delta State?
Yes! We regularly cater destination celebrations in Edo, Anambra, Rivers, and Lagos States. Dedicated logistics and mobile kitchen crew fees apply.
Can we schedule a food tasting session before booking?
Absolutely! For all Silver and Gold event packages, we host a complimentary tasting session for up to 3 people at our commercial kitchen so you can sample our Jollof, Banga soup, and protein glazes firsthand.
