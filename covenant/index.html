Who We Are

Rooted in the uncompromised Word of God.

Covenant Grace Chapel was planted in Delta State with a clear mandate: to raise a people of unshakeable character, deep spiritual intimacy with Christ, and tangible socio-economic influence in our communities.

We believe church is not just a Sunday routine; it is an empowering family where every member discovers their spiritual gifts, finds genuine healing, and thrives in practical daily living.

Our Mandate & Core Values

Word · Worship · Warfare · Welfare. We stand for practical compassion—feeding the needy, supporting widows, and funding youth education scholarships across Delta State.

What to Expect on Sunday

What should I wear to Sunday service?
Come as you are! You will see members dressed in traditional Nigerian attire (Agbada, George, Ankara), corporate suits, and smart casual clothes. We care about your heart, not your dress code.
Is there a place for my children during service?
Yes! Our Kingdom Kids church provides secure, fully staffed classrooms for toddlers, children, and pre-teens with trained teachers and background-checked caregivers.
How do I become a member or get baptized?
We hold a 3-week Discovery & Membership Class every month after second service. Water baptism by immersion is conducted on the final Saturday of every month.
