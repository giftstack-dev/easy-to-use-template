Our Heritage & Craft

Where African heritage meets modern haute couture.

"A garment made specifically for your body changes how you walk into any room in the world."

Founded in the heart of Delta State, Adaeze Couture began with a single vintage sewing machine and a burning commitment: to celebrate authentic Nigerian textiles—from handwoven Aso-Oke and vibrant George fabrics to rich Italian silks—with contemporary tailoring precision.

Every garment is drafted by hand from over 25 individual anatomical measurements, basted, fitted in person or via guided video consultation, and hand-finished with meticulous inner seams that last a lifetime.

The Bespoke Experience

Four steps to your signature fit

01

Consultation

02

03

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Common questions about our bespoke tailoring process, turnaround times, and nationwide delivery.

How long does bespoke tailoring take?
For casual and corporate wear, our standard turnaround is 7 to 10 business days. Traditional ceremonial outfits and Agbadas take 10 to 14 days. Bespoke bridal couture requires 4 to 8 weeks to allow for fabric sourcing, beading, and multiple fittings. Express rush services are available upon request.
Can I order if I am outside Delta State or abroad?
Yes! Over 40% of our clients reside in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, the UK, and North America. We provide a step-by-step video measurement guide and hold virtual consultations. Finished garments are shipped via DHL Express with door-to-door tracking.
Can I bring my own fabric or do you source it?
Both options are welcome! You are free to provide your own fabric (Lace, George, Ankara, Cashmere), or our design team can source genuine authentic textiles from our vetted textile partners in Nigeria and internationally.
What is your perfect fit guarantee?
Every garment created by Adaeze Couture comes with a 100% Fit Guarantee. If any alteration is needed after your fitting, our tailors will adjust it at zero extra charge until it sits on you with absolute perfection.
