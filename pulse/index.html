Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the free 1-day guest pass?
Your free 1-day guest pass gives you unlimited access to our strength training floor, cardio deck, locker rooms, showers, and any one scheduled group fitness class of your choice on that day.
I am a complete beginner. Is Pulse Fitness too intense for me?
Not at all! Over half of our members had never touched a barbell before joining. Our coaches scale every exercise to your current fitness level and teach proper posture before adding weight.
Can I freeze my membership if I travel abroad?
Yes, Quarterly and Annual VIP members can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year at no penalty when traveling for work or personal holidays.
