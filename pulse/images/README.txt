HOW TO ADD PHOTOS TO PULSE FITNESS LEKKI
=========================================
1. Save your fitness photos in this folder (e.g., hero.jpg, gym.jpg, trainer1.jpg, etc.).
2. Open style.css in the parent folder.
3. Locate the "PHOTO SLOTS" section near the top.
4. Replace the Picsum demo URL with your local photo file:
   For example, change:
     --photo-hero: url("https://picsum.photos/seed/pulse-hero/1600/1000");
   to:
     --photo-hero: url("images/hero.jpg");
5. Because every photo slot uses a CSS background layer with a branded gradient,
   your gym page will always look athletic and electric even before you add custom photos!
