Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical project timeline and process?
Complete brand identity and landing page sprints typically take 2 to 3 weeks. Comprehensive mobile app or SaaS platforms take 4 to 8 weeks depending on user flows, edge cases, and design system depth. I work in 1-week milestones with regular video demos.
Do you write code or hand off to engineers?
My core discipline is Figma design, but I write semantic HTML5 and modern CSS3. Every design is built with autolayout, responsive constraints, and design tokens that map 1:1 to Tailwind CSS or React components, ensuring painless handoffs.
What are your billing terms?
Projects are structured with a 50% upfront deposit to secure the sprint slot and 50% upon final delivery of all production Figma files. Payments can be settled in Nigerian Naira via bank transfer or USD/GBP via foreign wire.
