Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a template for free?
Yes, absolutely! All eight templates are open-source under the MIT license. You can download the ZIP files, customize them yourself, and host them on your own servers without paying anyone a single Kobo.
How do I host the files?
Because there are no complex databases or server runtimes, you can host them virtually anywhere for free:

1. GitHub Pages (100% Free): Upload the files to a repository on GitHub, turn on Pages in settings, and your site is live immediately.
2. Netlify: Simply drag and drop your unzipped template folder onto app.netlify.com/drop.
3. Shared Hosting / cPanel: Upload the files into your public_html directory via File Manager or FTP.
How do I change colors and text?
Open index.html in any text editor like Notepad. Every placeholder has a <!-- REPLACE --> comment next to it. To change colors, open style.css and edit the --primary, --accent, and background variables right at the top under the "EDIT YOUR BRAND HERE" section.
Do the pages work on phones?
Yes, 100%! Over 85% of Nigerian web traffic comes from smartphones. Every template is meticulously engineered with responsive CSS Grid, Flexbox, fluid clamp() typography, and CSS-only touch-friendly navigation bars tested down to 360px widths with zero horizontal scrolling.
Can you set it up for me?
Yes! If you prefer not to edit files yourself, click the "Request a Template" button or choose one of our setup packages in the pricing section above. We will handle the copywriting, branding, domain connection, and launch within 48 hours.
