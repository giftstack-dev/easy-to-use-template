Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-ins or is it appointment only?
While we accommodate walk-ins when a therapist is free, we strongly encourage booking in advance via WhatsApp to avoid waiting times, especially for weekend hair and spa services.
Do you offer on-location home or bridal services?
Yes! Our bridal glam team travels across Nigeria for weddings, engagements, and private group bookings. A dedicated travel logistics fee applies depending on the venue location.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept direct bank transfers, Nigerian debit cards (POS), and cash payments at our reception desk upon arrival.
