HOW TO ADD ASSETS TO LAUNCHPAD (LAUNCH)
========================================
1. Save your screenshots or graphics in this folder (e.g., hero.jpg, app_preview.jpg).
2. Open style.css in the parent folder.
3. Locate the "PHOTO SLOTS" section near the top.
4. Replace the Picsum demo URL with your local photo file:
   For example, change:
     --photo-hero: url("https://picsum.photos/seed/launch-hero/1600/1000");
   to:
     --photo-hero: url("images/hero.jpg");
5. Because Launchpad also features a rich pure-CSS interactive app mockup in the hero,
   your page looks futuristic, polished, and high-tech right out of the box!
