Frequently Asked Questions

Common Technical Questions

How fast do bank transfers settle into Nigerian bank accounts?
All transfers made through our payout endpoints settle in real-time via the Nigerian Inter-Bank Settlement System (NIBSS) instant payment rails, typically completing in under 2.4 seconds with immediate webhook confirmation.
Is my startup required to have a registered CAC certificate to test?
You can sign up and start testing with sandbox API keys immediately without company registration. To switch to live money mode and receive real customer payments, standard CAC registration and corporate bank account verification are required.
What security standards does Launchpad comply with?
All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 with AES-256 at rest. We adhere to NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) standards and enforce dual-factor authentication on all sensitive payout approvals.
